Events

Changes save to CPA's config.yaml automatically, and the plugin applies them within a few seconds. Text and numbers save when you leave the field or press Enter. Save results appear beside section headings or advanced group legends.

Accounts

Drag a handle to set the default order of quota cards and charts. Press Esc during a drag to cancel.

Names are saved as the first line of the CPA credential note; other lines are kept. Leave the name empty to distinguish accounts by email. Clearing a name on a multiline note requires confirmation to delete the whole note.

For services with several accounts, the total averages their remaining quota by weight. Weights must be above 0; decimals are allowed. Leave a field empty to use the plan default shown in it.

Alert rules

Alert thresholds
One notification when quota drops to each threshold, and one more when it runs out. The thresholds must not increase from first to third; the first two also set the colors of the bars and the chart.
5-hour window 7-day window Notify when quota recovers
Remind before resets

"After out": after the window runs out, notify once at its next reset. "If left": remind only when more than the Critical threshold is left. Reminders are sent 1 hour before a 5-hour window resets and 1 day before a 7-day window resets. Recovery notifications include what was left of the ended cycle, from the query 30 seconds before every reset.
